# Homepage surface brief

- Scope: `website/index.html`; visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience/job: agentic developers evaluating a native Mac environment for running and reviewing parallel isolated tasks.
- Primary action: download Workroom for macOS.
- Belief to earn: Workroom keeps concurrent development contexts isolated, visible, and reviewable in one terminal-first environment.
- Proof: the current annotated Workroom screenshot, repository-backed feature copy, Git/Jujutsu mechanisms, and real split/diff/file/VCS capabilities. The screenshot is approved but deliberately easy to replace soon.
- Constraints: static site; no beta, source-code, or repository-facing language; no invented metrics, customers, testimonials, pricing, dates, or persistence across app restarts; parent brand is Codaset; the square blocked mark is shared by Codaset and Workroom; responsive and accessible.

## Chosen direction

Universal Broadcast Service: a rigid lattice on black, smooth condensed broadcast type, fixed signal colors, a minimal brand/download masthead, and block mosaics. It keeps teletext's visual energy without requiring visitors to understand page numbers, HOLD/REVEAL vocabulary, or other medium-specific conventions. The memorable moment is the opening broadcast where “Every task gets its own room.” and the download action share the viewport with the real multi-workroom app proof.

Approved composition: `.impeccable/mocks/home-a-index.png` (A — Broadcast Index / balanced). Carry forward its side-by-side offer and product proof, factual ticker, and three-channel capability index. Also include the compact square yellow pixel mark from the earlier Teletext decision sketch as Workroom’s homepage signature.
