2. Key Technical Concepts:

A drawn shadow is clipped at the window bounds, so the window must be padded; SwiftUI `.clipShape` clips a view's `.background`, so a shadow must be a *second* `.background` applied after the clip; a CALayer `shadowPath` on an `NSViewRepresentable` does not render inside `NSHostingView`, but a SwiftUI shape's `.shadow` does.
