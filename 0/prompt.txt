### Why `gutter` can't just be reverted for the pane alone

`WorkroomPaneMetrics.gutter` is deliberately one shared constant across `WorkroomSplitView.swift:261`, `SidebarColumn.swift:46`, and `InspectorColumn.swift:39` specifically so the three side-by-side columns' top/bottom edges line up — `SidebarColumn.swift:41-44` documents a real, previously-fixed misalignment bug from exactly this kind of drift. The current uncommitted diff doesn't break that invariant (it shrank the one shared constant, so all three columns moved together, still aligned) — but it means the pane's bottom margin can't be nudged back up without either reverting `gutter` everywhere (undoing the inter-pane/split-gutter tightening too) or adding the extra margin in a place that also touches Sidebar/Inspector.

`WorkroomTerminalsView.swift:68` and `PaneTreeView.swift:437`, by contrast, are dual-purpose only at their own nesting level (terminal-split gutter for #437; nothing for #68) — #68 has zero inter-pane role and is freely revertible with no side effects.

## Recommendation

Don't revert any of the three values — that would re-widen the inter-pane/terminal-split gutters the edits were made for. Instead, add a new window-edge-only bottom margin **outside** `WorkroomSplitView`'s `GeometryReader`, mirroring how `outerGutter` already isolates its horizontal margin from the interior layout:

- `WorkroomSplitView.swift:110`: change `.padding(.horizontal, Self.outerGutter)` to also add `.padding(.bottom, WorkroomPaneMetrics.windowBottomMargin)` (new constant, value `4`). Padding applied outside the `GeometryReader` only shrinks the space `PaneTreeLayout.plan` lays leaves out in — it insets the *outer* boundary of the whole layout (solo or split) without touching the interior divider gaps that come from each leaf's own `gutter`/`.padding(1)`. So this restores the bottom margin without re-widening any inter-pane gutter.
- Mirror the same constant into `SidebarColumn.swift:46` and `InspectorColumn.swift:39`: `bottomMargin: WorkroomPaneMetrics.gutter + WorkroomPaneMetrics.windowBottomMargin`. Without this mirror, the pane card's bottom edge would sit higher than the sidebar/inspector's (still just `gutter`), reintroducing the exact misalignment `SidebarColumn.swift:41-44` describes fixing.
- With `windowBottomMargin = 4`: total = `gutter(2) + WorkroomTerminalsView pad(1) + PaneLeafView pad(1) + 4 = 8`, matching the original.

This is bottom-only by design, per what was actually reported — `gutter` shrank the *top* margin (under the title bar) identically, but that wasn't flagged, so leave it alone. Applying this fix will make each pane card's own top/bottom margins asymmetric (2pt top vs 8pt-equivalent bottom) and same for Sidebar/Inspector top vs bottom — a new asymmetry that didn't exist before, worth flagging to the user/reviewer even though it's out of this task's scope to resolve.</result>
